Summary

Milestone v1 replaces the v0.4.1 budget reservation model with a real funded
FPT payroll vault and three new settlement primitives.

1. Funded FPT custody vault

The contract keeps a real token ledger. faucet_or_deposit mints FPT one to one
against native GEN backing. transfer_tokens moves FPT between accounts. Every
payroll agreement moves its full maximum liability from the employer balance
into vault_custody_balance at creation, so work is never submitted against an
unfunded promise. Settlements write an immutable SettlementReceipt with the
worker payout, employer refund, payroll band, delivery anchor, and bond outcome.

2. PAPITO maximum liability lock

For every agreement, max_liability equals base_pay plus overtime_or_bonus_cap.
One hundred percent of that amount stays in vault custody through work
submission, the initial live audit, and the entire appeal window. The employer
cannot withdraw unearned surplus while a tranche is submitted, audited inside
its appeal window, or under appeal. recover_employer_surplus reverts with
"Appeal window open: liability locked" or "Appeal pending: liability locked"
until every tranche is final. The vault invariant is:

vault_custody_balance == sum(locked_payroll_escrows) + sum(active_appeal_bonds)

3. Two-party mutual settlement co-signing

propose_mutual_payroll_settlement accepts the exact same worker_payout_bps from
both the employer and the worker. A single signature only records a proposal
and moves zero funds. A mismatched second signature reverts with "Co-sign bps
mismatch". When both signatures match, the worker payout plus the employer
refund equal the full maximum liability, so one hundred percent of the escrow
is conserved.

4. Milestone tranche and consensus band audit

The worker submits a tranche with a sanitized timesheet and commit-pinned
evidence URL. The live audit snapshots every storage field into plain Python
locals before the non-det block, renders the evidence with
gl.nondet.web.render, and calls gl.eq_principle.prompt_comparative. The shared
helper _compute_payroll_settlement quantizes verified delivery onto the
discrete 0, 50, 80, 100 anchor ladder and returns one of four payroll bands:
FULL_BONUS_PAYOUT, STANDARD_TARGET_PAYOUT, PARTIAL_PRORATED_PAYOUT, or
REJECTED_NON_DELIVERY. Validators must agree exactly on payroll_band,
delivery_anchor, and evidence_authenticated. The contract then re-verifies the
anchor, the band, the authenticity flag, and the payout deterministically.
Malformed or prompt-injected JSON fails closed to anchor 0 and no payout.

Verification checklist

- genvm-lint check contracts/contract.py: lint and validation passed, 19 public
  methods (9 view, 10 write).
- Direct Mode suite: 179 passing tests.
- PAPITO regression: employer surplus withdrawal reverts while the appeal
  window is open and while an appeal is pending; recovery succeeds only after
  finality and never below the final worker payout.
- Mutual co-sign: single-party call does not settle and does not change the
  vault; matching employer and worker signatures settle; payout plus refund
  equals max liability; mismatched bps reverts.
- Band audit: all four discrete bands, score quantization at 0, 24, 25, 64,
  65, 89, 90, and 100, unauthenticated evidence forced to zero, malformed JSON
  fail-closed, prose-wrapped JSON accepted, and exact validator agreement
  replay.
- Vault invariant: checked after creation, submission, audit, appeal,
  resolution, surplus recovery, settlement, and mutual settlement, and across
  multiple agreements in mixed states.
- Evidence URL allowlist: four immutable IPFS gateways plus commit-pinned raw
  GitHub files, rejecting lookalike hosts, userinfo, ports, http, query,
  fragment, traversal, and branch-name URLs.
- Access control: no owner or admin storage field, no employer scoring or
  audit override, permissionless live audit and settlement, worker-only
  submission and appeal, employer-only surplus recovery, party-only co-sign.
